3.1QWIM Regime-Based Investing with LLMs

MSFE Capstone·HMM, SJM, LPPLS[Report]

Regime-aware portfolio management framework combining market-state detection (Hidden Markov [3] & Statistical Jump Models [6], in time and frequency domains) with LPPLS bubble-risk signals, feeding out-of-sample mean-variance construction against StaticMVO benchmarks. 3.3GARCH Option Pricing & IV Term Structure

FRE 6083·GARCH, Monte Carlo, Python[Report][Slides]

Compared implied-volatility term structures from Black–Scholes vs GARCH(1,1) simulation on S&P 500 options, with the conditional variance of Bollerslev [2],

σt2=ω+α εt−12+β σt−12,\sigma_t^2=\omega+\alpha\,\varepsilon_{t-1}^2+\beta\,\sigma_{t-1}^2,σt2​=ω+αεt−12​+βσt−12​,
(3)

where α\alphaα and β\betaβ govern, respectively, the reaction of σt2\sigma_t^2σt2​ to shocks and its persistence. 3D IV surfaces show BS overpricing deep OTM calls by up to 8.6%.
